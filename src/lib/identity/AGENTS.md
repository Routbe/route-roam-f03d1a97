# Rules
- KYC: `identity_verifications` (db/81) stores a peppered HMAC `unique_human_id` (UNIQUE), is immutable for `rout_app` once verified, and its real name overrides `display_name` server-side; Didit is capped monthly in `didit_monthly_usage`. Why: one account per person, no fake names, bounded cost.
- The blue identity mark and every public trust badge come only from `rout_public_identity_badges` (db/0282, Didit); `profiles.verified`/`is_paid` grant Pro and the separate supporter crown, never identity. Why: identity and payment stay strictly separate.
- Didit v3 attestations from src/lib/kyc store no real name (`verified_real_name` stays NULL) and use the primary universal nullifier as `unique_human_id`; name-based checks (0283 workspace IBAN holder match, display-name override) therefore do not apply to them and fail closed. Why: the owner's zero-plaintext directive of 2026-11-17 supersedes storing the document name.
- Green "Real Human" shield (`real_human`, `real_human_verified`, alias `human_linked`) comes exclusively from a zero-knowledge proof (0288 `evidence = 'zk_proof'`); the blue mark is "KYC Attested" (Didit/eIDAS) and a root profile that chose the green shield without such a proof shows the blue one. Why: owner directive of 2026-11-17 13:45 UTC; KYC, W Identity, manual review and payment never imply humanity badges.
- The blue-mark card is signed server-side with Ed25519 (src/lib/identity/badge-attestation*.ts, key HKDF'd from ROUT_SUBJECT_MASTER_KEY, public key at /api/badge/key) over exactly the shown handle, method, name, country and month, and verified only in the crypto worker op badge.verify; a minor owner's name is masked to first name + initial in readPublicProfile before signing (db-pending/0292 rout_public_owner_is_minor, fallback rout_user_is_minor). Why: the card text must be provably ROUT's, and a minor's full name must never leave the server.
